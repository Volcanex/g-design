The entire per-app override surface is four variables: --accent-fill, --accent-fill-hover, --accent-fg and --accent-edge, set on any scope. Every primary button, checkbox, switch, focus accent and brand mark follows with zero component changes. The danger button ignores all three brands on purpose: red and green carry meaning, not identity, and are never overridable.
