In-flow elements never cast drop shadows. Linear's treatment applies instead: a 1px alpha border plus an inset 0 1px 0 highlight reads as elevation without murk. Shadows are reserved for the two floating layers (menus, dialogs, toasts) that genuinely sit above the page. Translucency and glass are rejected: alpha is for composing borders and fills, not for blurring content behind surfaces.
