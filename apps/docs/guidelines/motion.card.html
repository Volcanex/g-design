The rules

ease-outDefault for everything; ease-in-out only for movement on screen
≤ 300msHard ceiling. Only the sheet (500ms) exceeds it
transform, opacityThe only animated properties without written justification
scale(0.97)Press feedback; nothing enters below scale(0.95)
no ambientNo scroll reveals, no ambient motion, none on frequent actions
interruptibleTransitions, not queued animations: five clicks is one movement
reduced motionFalls back to crossfades, never to nothing

When not to animate

frequentActions repeated many times an hour carry no motion: typing feedback, table row hover, plain navigation clicks
hoverHover states change colour and border, never position or size; the button's 2% growth is the single exception
ambientNothing moves because a surface looked static; motion answers an action or shows what changed

Named principles

anticipationHold-to-delete: the trash lid opens and the body wiggles before anything is destroyed
follow-throughThe bell's clapper trails its body by 60ms; toasts settle on a spring past their resting point

Springs

snap 200ms
smooth 300ms
sheet 500ms

Generated as CSS linear() from visualDuration and bounce at build time: zero JS at runtime, with an ease-out fallback where unsupported. Ad-hoc values are forbidden.
