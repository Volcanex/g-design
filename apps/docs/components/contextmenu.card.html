g.contextMenu(menu, x, y) is the open the data-context-menu attribute uses, for a surface that knows what was hit and fills the menu first: a heading names the thing, items that do not apply are disabled rather than removed, so the menu keeps its shape. Render the menu once and keep it. The menu key or shift F10 opens it at the focused thing.
