Kinds
Success and failure carry their icon; a plain toast is just the fact. Errors say what happened and what happens next, with no apology and no exception strings.
With an action
An action button rides the toast; Undo gets a longer timer. A loading toast is sticky until it resolves, then dismisses itself like any other.
Progress
A measurable job gets a filled bar and a percentage; the fill only moves forward. Work with no measurable end sweeps instead of lying about a number. The bar leaves with the loading state, so the resolved toast is just the outcome.
The stack
Newest lands on top; the rest recede at 14px and 5% scale per step, three visible. Hovering the stack pauses every timer. The word stays the same through the flow: a Publish button makes a Published toast.
