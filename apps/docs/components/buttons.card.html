Hierarchy
Primary is the far end of the neutral scale. Every neutral control keeps a visible border; no naked ghosts.
Meaning
Green commits, red destroys; always solid, never used for emphasis. Approve starts bare and draws its tick when clicked. Delete guards itself two ways, chosen from the pointer: on a mouse it is a held press, where the fill sweeps, the trash wiggles and releasing early rewinds it; on touch, where holding reads as a stuck tap, it is a second tap instead, "Delete" then "Really delete?". Both end in one hold-complete event. A view never holds both a contrast primary and a go.
Feedback buttons
The taxonomy: destructive buttons show their icon before, as a warning; feedback buttons earn theirs after. Save is the primary colour, never green, and its floppy springs in and blinks as it writes. Send carries its plane up front, then the plane flies and a check lands. While a download runs, arrows feed into the tray on a conveyor and it flexes as each lands. Refresh does one spring-timed revolution per click. Every label swap pins the width and resets its timer on re-click.
